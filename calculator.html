How We Estimate Your Trip

Our calculator uses preset destination rates to provide an approximate travel budget for planning purposes.

01

Select Your Destination

Choose from Bali, Thailand, Japan, Switzerland and Maldives. Each destination has its own estimated daily travel rates.

02

Enter Your Travel Details

Add the number of people travelling and the number of days. Accommodation is estimated using two travellers per room.

03

Choose Your Travel Style

Choose Budget, Standard or Luxury. Your selected style adjusts the final estimated package cost.
