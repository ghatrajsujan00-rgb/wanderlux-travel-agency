OUR STORY

Inspired by a Love for Travel

WanderLux Travel Agency is a newly established travel service provider created around a simple idea: every journey should be personal, enjoyable and memorable.

We understand that travellers have different interests, budgets and expectations. Some prefer quiet beach holidays, while others enjoy cultural discoveries, guided tours or exciting outdoor adventures.

WanderLux aims to make holiday planning easier by bringing destination information, travel services, budget estimation and consultation requests together in one convenient website.

PERSONAL TRAVEL SUPPORT

Meet Our Travel Consultants

The WanderLux concept includes dedicated consultant roles designed to support customers throughout their travel planning.

DP

Destination Planning

Travel Destination Specialist

This role focuses on helping travellers compare destinations, explore attractions and choose suitable holiday experiences based on their interests.

HP

Holiday Planning

Personalised Package Specialist

This role focuses on helping customers explore holiday packages, accommodation options and travel preferences to develop a suitable itinerary.

CS

Customer Support

Travel Enquiry Coordinator

This role focuses on responding to general travel enquiries, managing appointment requests and guiding customers towards relevant travel information.

These profiles describe proposed consultant responsibilities for the WanderLux website concept and do not represent verified individual employees.
